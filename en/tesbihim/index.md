# Tesbihim - Dhikr Counter

Tesbihim is a free digital tesbih (dhikr counter): no ads, no account, no tracking, and every feature is free. It is designed accessibility-first with VoiceOver, so it works just as well for blind and low-vision users as it does for sighted users.
{: .lead}

{% include app-links.html slug="tesbihim" languages="English, Turkish, Arabic, Bengali, Indonesian, Malay, Urdu" %}

## What you can do

- Count freely with no target using Serbest Tesbih, or pick a preset dhikr with a built-in goal (Sübhanallah, Elhamdülillah, Allahu Ekber, Salavat and more).
- Create your own custom dhikr, search your list, and pin your favorites to the top.
- Restore any dhikr you removed from the Removed section at any time.
- Progress is saved separately for each dhikr: your count and place are kept when you switch dhikrs or reopen the app, until you reset them.

## Accessible counting for everyone

- Tap anywhere on the counting area to count: double-tap with VoiceOver on, single tap with it off.
- A dedicated fast-counting mode, built for VoiceOver users, lets a single tap anywhere on screen add one to the count.
- Made a mistake? Use Decrease to undo, or Reset to zero the current count.
- The My Dhikrs screen shows the count and target of each dhikr, and VoiceOver reads this too.

## Goals and feedback

- Choose whether reaching your goal stops the count or cycles it back to zero for another round.
- Set a milestone interval (for example every 33) to get a distinct vibration and sound at each checkpoint.

## History and stats

- Review your daily, weekly and monthly dhikr history with charts and a day-by-day breakdown.
- See how much of each dhikr you have done and how many goals you have completed.

## Make it yours

- Customize sound, vibration and appearance (light or dark theme).
- Set a reminder per dhikr, or a General Reminder that is not tied to any single dhikr.

## Languages

The interface is available in English, Turkish, Arabic, Bengali, Indonesian, Malay and Urdu (7 languages).

## Price and privacy

- The app is free and every feature is open to everyone.
- No account is needed; there are no ads and no tracking.
- If you like it, you can optionally support the developer from Settings (there are three amounts to choose from). Support never unlocks or locks any feature.
