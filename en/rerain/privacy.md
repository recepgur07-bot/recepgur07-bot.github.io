# ReRain privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026.

ReRain uses no advertising, advertising tracking or analytics tools. Forali does not collect data about your use of the app. You don't need an account.

## Information on your device

Your stars, unlocked levels, high score, game statistics, unfinished game and settings are stored on your device. This information is not sent to Forali.

## iCloud sync

If iCloud sync is on, your stars, unlocked levels, record and game statistics carry over to your devices on the same Apple Account through your own iCloud account. No personal information is synced. This data is stored by Apple in your account; Forali cannot see it. You can turn sync off in ReRain Settings › Data. With sync on, "Reset progress" also erases progress on your other devices.

## Notifications

The daily reminder is off by default. If you turn it on, the notification is scheduled locally on your device; no information is sent to any server. You can change notification permission in your device's Settings app.

## Purchases

Apple processes the Full Version, Sound Pack, Complete Bundle and tip purchases. Forali does not see your payment or Apple Account details. Apple's processing is subject to the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support and contact

A support email only includes the app version, device model and iOS version automatically. If you send an email, we use your address, message and anything you include only to answer your request. Please don't send personal information that isn't needed. [Email ReRain support](mailto:support@forali.app).

## Children

ReRain is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
