# Kelimelerim: Learn Vocabulary

Kelimelerim turns learning English words into a game: 13,629 words across all six CEFR levels from A1 to C2, six game modes and unlimited personal lists. Words that commonly appear in Turkish exams such as YDS, YÖKDİL and YDT are included. The app interface is in Turkish, and it works with VoiceOver and spoken word playback.

## Learn through play

- **Quiz:** test yourself with quick 4-option questions.
- **Marathon:** keep going until your first mistake; choose 10, 20 or 30 seconds per question.
- **Timed Challenge:** see how many words you know in 60 seconds.
- **Match:** pair words with their meanings.
- **Word Scramble:** put the mixed letters in the right order.
- **Word Hunt:** build words letter by letter from a grid.

## Your own word pool

- Add words with meaning, level, part of speech and an optional English definition.
- Bulk-import a word list via CSV; preview it first, then add it to your library.
- Create as many lists as you want; your lists work across every game.
- Narrow each session by level, word source or your lists, and add words you struggled with to a study list.

## Accessibility

The interface is in Turkish. The app is designed for studying at your own pace with VoiceOver support, spoken word playback and adjustable voice settings.
