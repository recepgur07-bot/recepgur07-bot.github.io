# News

We announce new apps, major updates and changes to this site here. The newest announcement is at the top.

## 24 October 2026: Tesbihim and OneDay pages

Pages for our dhikr counter [Tesbihim](/en/tesbihim/) and our countdown app [OneDay](/en/oneday/) are now online.

## 23 October 2026: Folio 1.2.1

[Folio](/en/folio/) 1.2.1 lets you choose a separate sound for each event and your currency, adds a new Income template, and opens phone numbers, email and web addresses with one tap. VoiceOver actions now come in the same order in every notebook.

## 21 October 2026: Harfoni is out

Our accessibility-focused Turkish word game [Harfoni](/en/harfoni/) is now on the App Store for iPhone and iPad: [view Harfoni on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759314762). The game is Turkish only; the site also has a [user guide](/en/harfoni/guide/).

## 20 October 2026: Bellball is out

Our audio-based penalty game [Bellball](/en/bellball/) is now on the App Store for iPhone and iPad: [view Bellball on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6821299495). The site also has a [user guide](/en/bellball/guide/) and a privacy page.

## 19 October 2026: ReSort 1.1.0

[ReSort](/en/resort/) 1.1.0 adds seven ranks, a choice of sound for every game moment, background music, iCloud sync and a larger tube size. The sound and music packs are one-time purchases; gameplay and accessibility features are open to everyone.

## 8 October 2026: ReSort is out

Our calm, ad-free water sort puzzle [ReSort](/en/resort/) is now on the App Store for iPhone and iPad: [view ReSort on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6819550779). There are no timers or move limits, and it is designed to be played by touch, with a keyboard or with VoiceOver.

## 8 October 2026: New help and privacy pages

We added [Our privacy principles](/en/privacy/), [Frequently asked questions](/en/faq/) and [Press and media](/en/press/) pages, and grouped our apps into categories. [Kelimelerim](/en/kelimelerim/) also has its own page now.

## 7 October 2026: Forali has a new address

The site that brings our apps together now lives at forali.app. Old addresses redirect here. For support, write to [support@forali.app](mailto:support@forali.app); for general enquiries, [hello@forali.app](mailto:hello@forali.app).

## 5 October 2026: FrameMate user guide

Our Mac screen recorder [FrameMate](/en/framemate/) now has a step-by-step guide for using it with VoiceOver.

## 27 September 2026: Memora and Folio pages

Our photo gallery [Memora](/en/memora/) got its own page and VoiceOver user guide, and our tracking notebook app [Folio](/en/folio/) got its own page.
