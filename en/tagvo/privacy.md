# Tagvo privacy policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026.

Tagvo doesn't require an account and doesn't use ads, ad tracking or analytics. Forali doesn't collect data about how you use the app.

## Information on your device

Your items' names, places, notes and your settings are stored only on your iPhone and are included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer backup). Tagvo doesn't send this information to any server.

## NFC tags

Tagvo writes only the item's identifier to a tag: a meaningless string of letters and numbers. The item's name, note or place is not stored on the tag. Another phone reading the tag can't see your item's name.

## Microphone and voice notes

The microphone is used only while you record a voice note for an item. Voice notes are stored on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere. Reading aloud uses your device's own voices or VoiceOver.

## Backup file

The "Prepare backup" button creates a single file with your items and voice notes on your iPhone. You decide where to save it and who to share it with.

## Siri and Shortcuts

The "Where is" and scan shortcuts run on your iPhone. When Siri tells you where an item is, it gets the information from the app; Forali doesn't see these requests.

## Tips

Tagvo is free. The three optional tips don't unlock any features. Payments are handled by Apple; Forali doesn't see your payment or Apple account details. Apple's processing is covered by the [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support and contact

If you email support, we use your address, your message and the app version, device model and iOS version added to the email automatically only to answer your request. Please don't include personal information that isn't needed. [Email Tagvo support](mailto:support@forali.app).
