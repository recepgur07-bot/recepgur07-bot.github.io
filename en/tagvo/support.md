# Tagvo support

If you have a question about Tagvo or run into a problem, [email Forali support](mailto:support@forali.app). In the app you can use "Send support email" in Settings; the app version, device model and iOS version are added to the email automatically. Please don't include personal information that isn't needed.

## Frequently asked questions

Which tags work? NTAG213, NTAG215 and NTAG216 tags work. Tags stuck on metal are hard to read; metal surfaces need special tags.

What happens to my items when I change phones? Tags hold only an identifier; your items' names stay on the phone. Before changing phones, use "Prepare backup" in Settings and save the file to Files, then use "Restore from backup" on the new phone. Restoring an iPhone backup also brings your items over.

What do tips do? Tagvo is free; tips don't unlock any features, they only support development.

[Read the Tagvo privacy policy](/en/tagvo/privacy/).
