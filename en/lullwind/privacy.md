# Lullwind privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026.

Lullwind uses no advertising, advertising tracking or analytics tools. Forali does not collect data about your use of the app.

## Information on your device

Your settings, mixes and voice recordings are stored on your device. The microphone is used only after you tap Record and grant permission. Voice recordings are not sent to Forali. You can change microphone permission in your device's Settings app.

Battery level is read on your device only to show a charging reminder and to stop the sound when the battery is very low; it is not stored or sent.

## iCloud sync

When iCloud sync is on, your mixes, favorites and settings sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac with your own iCloud account. Voice recordings are copied to the app's area in your iCloud Drive only if you turn on "Also copy parent recordings to iCloud" in Settings. Apple stores this data in your account; Forali cannot see it. You can turn sync off in Lullwind Settings.

## Notifications

The bedtime reminder is off by default. If you turn it on, the notification is scheduled locally on your device; nothing is sent to any server.

## Purchases

Apple handles purchases and free trials. Forali does not see your payment or Apple account information. Apple's processing is subject to the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support and contact

If you email support, we use your address, message and any information you include to answer your request. Please do not send personal information that is not needed. [Email Lullwind support](mailto:support@forali.app).
