# Lullwind support

If you have a question about Lullwind or encounter a problem, [email Forali support](mailto:support@forali.app). Please describe the issue and, if possible, include your device model and iOS or iPadOS version. Do not include personal information that is not needed.

You can manage microphone permission in your device's Settings app. Purchases are managed through your Apple account.

For all-night use, turn on Do Not Disturb or the Sleep Focus so notification sounds don't wake you, and plug in your device for long winds. If the battery gets very low, the sound fades out and stops; an interruption such as a call pauses it.

[Read the Lullwind privacy policy](/en/lullwind/privacy/).
