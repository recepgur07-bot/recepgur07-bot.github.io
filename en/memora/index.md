# Memora: Photo Gallery

Find what you are looking for among thousands of photos and videos, and organize your library in bulk. In Memora, accessibility is not a patch added later; it is the foundation. Gestures such as swiping and pinching have named VoiceOver actions or button equivalents, and buttons say what they do.

## Privacy and price

- Memora does not copy, move or upload your photos. Your library stays in Apple Photos; Memora reads it where it is.
- Text recognition and image descriptions run on your device. No image is sent anywhere.
- The app is completely free: no ads, no account, no subscription, no analytics and no locked features.

## What you can do

### Find

- Narrow your library to All, Photos, Videos, Screenshots or Favorites, or browse inside a single album.
- Jump to today, yesterday, the last 7 days, this month, last month, a specific day or a date range.
- Use the "Date groups" rotor to jump between days and months without passing every photo in between.
- Search the names, tags, notes and descriptions you added, and the text inside your photos.

### Understand

- Hear each item's type, date, orientation, duration, name, tags, note, description and the text in the photo, in one steady order.
- Hear the text in your photos, recognized on your device, and copy it.
- Add a short on-device image description, or paste a description you got from another app.

### Organize

- Give photos your own names, notes and tags; shared files carry the name you gave.
- Select all photos of a day from its date header in one step, then share, name, tag or delete them.
- Create a multi-page PDF from your selection, or make smaller copies.
- Deleting always asks twice; deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where you can recover them.

### Make it yours

- Choose how much of the text, tags, notes and descriptions VoiceOver reads in the list.
- Reorder the rotor actions on items, and turn off the ones you do not need.
- Export the names and tags you added to a file and bring them to a new device.
