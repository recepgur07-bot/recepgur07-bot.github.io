# Accessibility

Our goal is to make everyday tools useful for everyone. We consider screen-reader access, clear navigation and readable interfaces from the start of development. For us, accessibility is not an add-on; it is the foundation of the design.

## Our commitment

- Accessibility features are never placed behind a paid upgrade in any of our apps.
- We design new features together with VoiceOver, keyboard and larger text use.
- We treat reported accessibility problems as a priority.

## In the apps

- Apps with a detailed page list their supported devices and system version there; for the others, this information is on the App Store page.
- Apps with a user guide explain step by step how to use them with VoiceOver.
- Every app is different; accessibility level and language support vary from app to app. We don't present unverified features as promises.

## On this site

- Every page has a single main heading and ordered subheadings, so you can navigate by headings. Pages have separate main menu, main content and footer regions.
- The "Skip to content" link at the top of each page skips the menu. The page you are on is announced as the "current page" in the menu.
- The site can be used with a keyboard alone, and the focused item is shown with a clear outline.
- Links are underlined, and link text makes sense when read on its own. Every app card includes the app's App Store link.
- Text follows your browser's text size, and pages reflow without horizontal scrolling when zoomed or on narrow screens.
- The light or dark appearance follows your system. Increased contrast and forced colors settings (such as Windows High Contrast) are respected, and so is the reduce motion preference.
- Turkish and English pages link to each other, and phrases in another language are marked so that screen readers pronounce them correctly.
- Expandable sections use only the browser's built-in disclosure element, and essential information is never hidden inside one.
- There are no cookies, analytics, tracking, external fonts or external scripts, and pages work without scripts.

## Target and evaluation

- Our target is for this site to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. This is a goal, not a formal conformance claim.
- The site goes through automated checks before every release: single main heading, heading order, page language, broken links and language counterparts.
- Colour contrast, narrow-screen layout and keyboard navigation are checked by hand in a browser.
- Last evaluated: 8 October 2026.

## Known limitations

- The site is currently available in Turkish and English. The languages each app supports may differ.
- Some apps' privacy and support pages live at separate addresses and don't use this site's shared design yet.
- Thorough testing of the site with screen readers on real devices has not been completed yet. For that reason we don't claim the site is fully accessible, and we want to hear about any problem you find.

## Report a problem

If you run into an accessibility problem in an app or on this site, please write to [support@forali.app](mailto:support@forali.app) or use the other options on the [Support and contact](/en/contact/) page. Telling us the page or app, your device and the assistive technology you use helps us respond faster.
