# Accessibility

Our goal is to make everyday tools useful for everyone. We consider screen-reader access, clear navigation and readable interfaces from the start of development. For us, accessibility is not an add-on; it is the foundation of the design.

## Our commitment

- Accessibility features are never placed behind a paid upgrade in any of our apps.
- We design new features together with VoiceOver, keyboard and larger text use.
- We treat reported accessibility problems as a priority.

## In the apps

- Apps with a detailed page list their supported devices and system version there; for the others, this information is on the App Store page.
- Apps with a user guide explain step by step how to use them with VoiceOver.
- Every app is different; accessibility level and language support vary from app to app. We don't present unverified features as promises.

## On this site

- Every page has a single main heading and ordered subheadings, so you can navigate by headings.
- The "Skip to content" link at the top of each page skips the menu.
- The site works with a keyboard, and the focused item is shown with a clear outline.
- Text follows your browser's text size, and the light or dark appearance follows your system.
- The reduce motion preference is respected.
- There are no cookies, analytics, tracking, external fonts or external scripts.

## Known limitations

- The site is currently available in Turkish and English. The languages each app supports may differ.
- Some older privacy and support pages don't use this site's shared design yet.

## Report a problem

If you run into an accessibility problem in an app or on this site, please report it through the options on the [Support and contact](/en/contact/) page.
