# Folio: Tracking Notebooks

Turn anything you need to keep track of into a notebook you can actually use: groceries and errands, bills and subscriptions, medication and habits, books to read. Over 30 ready-made templates, each with its own fields, statuses and reminders.
{: .lead}

{% include app-links.html slug="folio" %}

## Ready-made templates

- **Finance:** Payments, Expenses, Subscriptions, Debt/Credit
- **Content:** To Read, To Watch, To Listen
- **Special days and travel:** Birthdays, Places to Go, Packing
- **Routines:** Habits, Chores, Maintenance, Plant Care, Pet Care, Exercise
- **Processes:** Applications, Project Tracking, Awaiting, Orders, Loans
- **Personal tracking:** Inventory, Meal Plan, Courses, Goals, Attendance, Vaccinations
- Shopping, To-Do, and a completely blank notebook to start from scratch

## Design it your way

- Customize each notebook's fields, statuses and order with the Field Designer.
- Build your own templates, then use or remove them from the template picker.
- Organize notebooks into folders and pin the ones that matter most.

## Dates, reminders and recurrence

- Add a date, time and reminder to any item.
- Set daily, weekly, monthly, yearly or completion-based recurrence rules.
- Overdue items automatically surface to the top.

## Hands-free with Siri

- "Add to Folio": Siri asks which notebook and what to add, then keeps going with "Anything else?".
- "What's on tomorrow in Folio", "What's next in Folio", "What did I do yesterday in Folio".
- "When did I last do it in Folio": for medication, watering plants or feeding pets.
- "Read what's left in Folio": long lists are read five at a time.

## History, trash and sync

- Save a snapshot of completed lists to history and review them anytime.
- Deleted notebooks and items wait in Trash for a while, so you can bring them back.
- No account needed: Folio uses the iCloud account already on your device. Notebooks sync across your devices; nothing is stored on Folio's own servers.

## Accessibility

- Every screen and action is announced clearly with VoiceOver.
- Status changes, delete and undo, and list updates are announced instantly.
- Haptic and sound feedback can be customized in Settings.

## Price

Your first 2 notebooks are free forever and fully editable, with no payment info and no time limit. Monthly, yearly or one-time Lifetime Folio Pro unlocks unlimited notebooks, custom templates, folders and full JSON backup. Plain text and CSV export are always free.
