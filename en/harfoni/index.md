# Harfoni

Harfoni is a Turkish word game designed with accessibility-first gameplay. Build meaningful words from the given letters and move forward chapter by chapter.
{: .lead}

{% include app-links.html slug="harfoni" %}

The game is in Turkish: the words you build are Turkish words.

## What you can do

- A single forward path of chapters that keeps growing with updates.
- A new Daily Puzzle every day, a daily streak and a reminder at the time you choose.
- Anagram: use every letter to find the word.
- 90-second Time Race mode.
- Every word earns points; climb ranks as your score grows.
- Hints earned by playing, never sold.
- Common target words, bonus words from a large dictionary and a meaning for every word.

## Playable for everyone

Fully playable with VoiceOver, plus a touch area made for playing without sight.

## Price

- The first 25 chapters, the first 25 anagrams, the Daily Puzzle and 3 Time Race rounds a day are free.
- A one-time Full Version unlocks every chapter and unlimited rounds; no subscription, no ads.
