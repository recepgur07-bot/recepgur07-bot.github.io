# PipTalk privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026.

PipTalk has no ads, ad tracking or analytics SDKs. Forali does not send your gameplay activity to its own servers; Forali runs no server for this game.

## Information stored on your device

Settings, progress, statistics, unfinished games, tournament and league records, lesson progress and the free version's daily match counter stay only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them too.

## Playing with friends through Game Center

Playing with friends is optional and requires signing in to Apple Game Center. Players at the table can see your Game Center display name. During a game, your moves, the match state and your quick reactions are sent through Game Center to the other players' devices. Forali does not collect this information on a server. Your Game Center account and Apple's service operations are subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Games against bots and single-player games do not require Game Center.

## Push-to-talk radio and the microphone

The microphone is used only while you hold the radio button, and the game asks for your permission first. The short voice message is sent through Game Center only to the players at the table and is deleted from your device right after it is sent. Voice messages are never sent to Forali and are not stored anywhere. If you choose to open the radio only to your Game Center friends, your friends list is read from Apple on your device for that setting; it is not sent to Forali.

## Purchases

The Full Version, the trial and support tips go through Apple's App Store; Apple handles your payment and account details, and Forali never sees them. The game learns which products you own from Apple on your device.

## When you contact us

If you choose to email support, your address, your message and anything you attach are used only to answer your request. Please do not send personal information that is not needed.

## Managing your data

You can turn off microphone access in the Settings app at any time; the game works without the radio. You can manage your Game Center account in the Game Center section of the Settings app.

## Contact

For privacy questions, [email Forali support](mailto:support@forali.app).
