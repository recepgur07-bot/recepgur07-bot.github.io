# Lingtile privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026.

Lingtile needs no account and uses no ads, ad tracking or analytics. Forali does not collect data about how you use the app.

## Information on your device

Your courses, learning history, lists, your own words, notes and settings are stored on your device. Data moved over from Kelimelerim is processed only on your device. Read-aloud uses your device's built-in voices; no text is sent to any server.

## iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress and settings sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. Apple stores this data in your account; Forali cannot see it.

## Export

When you export your words or data, the file is created on your device. You decide whom to share it with.

## Notifications

The daily reminder and mini questions are off by default. If you turn them on, notifications are scheduled locally on your device; no information is sent to any server.

## Purchases

Apple handles purchases and free trials. Forali does not see your payment or Apple account details. Apple's processing is covered by the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support and contact

If you email support (from Settings or by shaking your phone), we use your address, your message and the app version, device model and iOS version added to the email only to answer your request. Please don't include personal information that isn't needed. [Email Lingtile support](mailto:support@forali.app).
