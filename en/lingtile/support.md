# Lingtile support

If you have a question about Lingtile or run into a problem, [email Forali support](mailto:support@forali.app). In the app you can use "Send support email" in Settings or shake your phone; the app version, device model and iOS version are added to the email automatically. Please don't include personal information that isn't needed.

Purchases are managed through your Apple account. Purchases made in Kelimelerim carry over to Lingtile; if yours doesn't show, use "Restore purchases".

[Read the Lingtile privacy policy](/en/lingtile/privacy/).
