# OneDay: Countdown & Widget

Create countdowns for birthdays, anniversaries and all your important days with OneDay. Use countdown, reminders and widgets in one app.
{: .lead}

{% include app-links.html slug="oneday" languages="Interface in 14 languages" %}

## What you can do

- **Future and Past tabs:** see how many days are left until upcoming events and how many days ago past events happened.
- **Notifications:** get reminders 30 days, 7 days and 1 day before, and on the event day, at the time you choose.
- **Yearly repeat:** for birthdays and anniversaries, use "repeat yearly" to see which year it is and how many days are left until the next date.
- **Widgets:** see your events on the home screen and lock screen without opening the app.
- **Personalization:** choose one of 12 colors, add one of 24 tags and add photos from your gallery.
- **Pin and filter:** keep important events at the top of the list and filter by color, tag or pinned status.
- **Sharing:** share your events as colorful cards.
- **Editing:** edit, delete or change any event.

## Accessibility

OneDay was built with VoiceOver support: buttons, fields and content were prepared with VoiceOver use in mind.

## Languages

The interface is available in 14 languages: Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Hindi and Indonesian.

## Price and privacy

- The app is free; the free version lets you add 5 events.
- A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited events with Premium; there is no subscription.
- Your data stays on your device; optional iCloud sync can keep your devices in step.
- There are no ads and no tracking.
