# Bellball privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Bellball has no ads, ad tracking or analytics SDKs. Forali does not send your gameplay activity to its own servers.

## Information stored on your device

Settings, career progress, local achievements, match history and game logs stay on your device. The game does not record your microphone. Audio output latency information is used locally for gameplay timing.

## Multiplayer through Game Center

Multiplayer is optional and requires signing in to Apple Game Center. Your opponent can see your Game Center display name. Shot, result, pause and connection messages are sent to the other player's device during a match. Forali does not collect this information on a server. Your Game Center account and Apple's service operations are subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Local play does not require Game Center. In this version, local achievements are not submitted to Game Center.

## When you contact us

If you choose to email support, your email address, message and information you include are used to answer your request. Game logs are not sent automatically; you choose whether to share them. Please do not send personal information that is unnecessary for your request.

## Managing your data

You can reset your career and restore default settings in the Data section of Settings. These actions ask for confirmation in the game. You can manage your Apple account in Game Center settings.

## Contact

For privacy questions, [email Forali support](mailto:support@forali.app).
