# Bellball: Audio Soccer Penalty

Hear the shot. Choose your corner. Make the save. Bellball brings soccer penalties to your ears on iPhone and iPad: take turns keeping goal and shooting, with sound guiding every move.
{: .lead}

{% include app-links.html slug="bellball" guide="/en/bellball/guide/" languages="Interface in 16 languages" %}

Play without looking at the screen, with VoiceOver support. Headphones make the direction of each shot clearer.

## Save by sound

Listen for left, center or right, move your keeper and react before the ball reaches the goal. Choose Columns, Rows or Flick controls for defense. Swipe down to shoot, then swipe left, up or right to choose your corner.

## Build your club career

- Choose a fictional club in the leagues of 36 countries.
- Compete in league matches, national cups and continental tournaments.
- Progress through a skill system with 25 levels, collect trophies and follow your match and zone statistics.

## Find your next match

- Play a friendly, enter a 32-team friendly tournament or challenge a friend through Game Center.
- Training, pitch study and a step-by-step tutorial help you learn the sounds and controls.
- Local play needs no app account or Game Center sign-in; online head-to-head matches require Game Center.

## The stadium in your ears

Commentary recordings are available in 13 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, German, Arabic, Italian, Korean, French, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese and Indonesian. Crowd ambience, chants and goal celebrations add to the match atmosphere. Commentary and extra stadium sounds are included in the Sound Pack, the trial and the introductory access described below.

## Price

Bellball is free to download.

- For the first 3 days, you get 7 friendly matches, 7 head-to-head matches and one new tournament per day, with all sounds.
- Afterwards, you get 2 friendly matches and 3 head-to-head matches per day, and can start a new tournament every 7 days.
- The first half of each career season remains free, with all sounds. Training and pitch study have no limits.
- Start a 7-day free trial once to unlock the full game and all sounds. When it ends, free play continues and your career and records are kept. No automatic charge or subscription starts.

In-app purchases are one-time, with no subscription:

- **Full Version:** removes the gameplay limits.
- **Sound Pack:** unlocks commentary and extra stadium sounds.
- **Complete Pack:** includes both.
- Head-to-head matches have no daily limit when either player has full-game access.
- Optional support tips unlock no content.

## Accessibility and privacy

VoiceOver support, announcements and gameplay-critical sounds are always available without a purchase. No ads, ad tracking or analytics SDKs. Forali does not collect your gameplay on its own servers. Apple handles purchases, Game Center and iCloud; match messages are exchanged with your opponent. Details: [Bellball privacy policy](/en/bellball/privacy/).
