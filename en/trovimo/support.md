# Trovimo support

Trovimo is a match-3 puzzle game you can play from start to finish with VoiceOver.

## Contact

For questions, problems or ideas, [email Forali support](mailto:support@forali.app). You can also write from inside the game in Settings. Please tell us your device, your iOS version and what happened.

## Restoring purchases

The Game Pack, the Sound Pack and the Game and Sound Pack are one-time purchases. On a new device, open Purchase and choose Restore purchases. Tips unlock no content. There is no subscription.

## Privacy

Trovimo collects no personal data. Read the [Trovimo privacy policy](/en/trovimo/privacy/).
