# Trovimo privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Trovimo collects no personal data. It has no ads, no ad tracking, no analytics SDKs and no account. Forali runs no server for this game.

## Information stored on your device

Your settings, progress, stars, stamps, daily puzzle results and sound choices stay on your device. Deleting the app deletes them too.

## iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your settings and progress can sync between your own devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This data goes only to your iCloud account; Forali cannot see it. iCloud is subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Purchases

The Game Pack, the Sound Pack, the Game and Sound Pack and support tips go through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment and account details, and Forali never sees them. The game learns which products you own from Apple on your device.

## When you contact us

If you choose to email support, your address, your message and anything you attach are used only to answer your request. Please do not send personal information that is not needed.

## Contact

For privacy questions, [email Forali support](mailto:support@forali.app).
